A 104-word monologue spoken by a dark lord who has ruled his valleys for three hundred years and greeted one hero too many. He still has a scheme — the eclipse, the bell, the old bargain — and the sword is polished, the trapdoor oiled, the monologue rehearsed. But the weariness leaks out in the clipped sentences, the deflections, and the small domestic wish at the end: he would like this over before the kettle boils. The tiredness lives entirely in the voice, never in a plain statement of it.

## Run it

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This prints the monologue and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
